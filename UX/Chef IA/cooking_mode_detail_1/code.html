PREMIUM RECIPE

Miso-Glazed Roasted Salmon & Greens

PREP

15 min

COOK

25 min

CALORIES

480 kcal

Ingredients

shopping_basket Check Pantry
Fresh Salmon
Miso Paste
Baby Bok Choy
Sesame Oil
Mirin

Step-by-Step

01
Active Step

Prepare the Miso Glaze

In a small bowl, whisk together the miso paste, mirin, honey, and sesame oil until the mixture is smooth and glossy.

timer 3:00 minute timer
02

Marinate the Salmon

Place the salmon fillets in a shallow dish. Pour the glaze over the fillets, ensuring they are evenly coated. Let them rest for at least 10 minutes.

03

Roast the Bok Choy

Toss the halved bok choy with a splash of oil and sea salt. Arrange on a baking sheet and roast until the edges are crispy and golden.
